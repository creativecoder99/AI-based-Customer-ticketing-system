# Shipping & Transit Policy

## Overview
This document details delivery timeframes, tracking, lost in transit claims, and shipping modifications.

## Standard Delivery Times
- Standard domestic shipments arrive within **3 to 5 business days** following warehouse fulfillment.
- Remote locations may require up to 7 to 10 business days.

## Address Modifications & Route Changes
- Address alterations are permitted only while order status displays "Processing" or "Pending Dispatch".
- Once the package is marked "Dispatched" or handed over to the courier partner, addresses cannot be changed mid-transit.

## Lost In Transit Claims
- An in-transit shipment is classified as "Lost in Transit" if tracking records indicate **no scan movement or carrier updates for 7 consecutive business days**.
- In such cases, customer support must immediately initiate courier escalation and dispatch a priority replacement (`EXPEDITE_SHIPPING`) or issue a full refund (`APPROVE_REFUND`) per customer preference.

## Missing Carrier Information
- Where queries report delivery delay but fail to supply the tracking number, order identifier, or delivery address, the agent must solicit required details (`NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION`).
