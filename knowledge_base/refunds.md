# Refunds Policy

## Overview
This policy governs how refunds are evaluated, processed, and disbursed to customers.

## Refund Timelines & Methods
- Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment within **5 to 7 business days** following warehouse receipt and physical inspection.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) refunds are remitted via bank NEFT / UPI transfer upon provision of verified IFSC details.

## Eligibility Conditions
- A refund is approved (`APPROVE_REFUND`) once an authorized return is received in satisfactory condition, or if an order cancellation is submitted prior to carrier pickup.
- If an item was delivered completely non-functional or ruined and satisfies the low-value threshold (≤ ₹2,000), immediate refund is permitted.

## Non-Refundable Surcharges
- Outbound shipping fees, expedited delivery surcharges, and gift packaging fees are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.
- Payment gateway convenience fees (if applicable) are non-refundable.

## Disputed or Unverified Claims
- Claims lacking order numbers, payment receipts, or where records indicate chargeback disputes already pending with the payment provider must request clarification (`NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION`).
