# Damaged Goods Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines the procedure for reporting and resolving items received in a damaged, broken, or defective condition.

## Reporting Timeframe
- All damage claims must be filed within **48 hours of confirmed delivery** by the carrier.
- Claims submitted after the 48-hour window has expired are ineligible for damage compensation and must be rejected (`REJECT_REQUEST`).

## Evidence & Photo Requirements
- **Orders Above ₹2,000**: For any damaged item or order with an item value exceeding ₹2,000, high-resolution photographs of the damaged item and the shipping container with shipping label visible are strictly mandatory (`REQUEST_PHOTOS`). No replacement or refund can be approved without this photographic evidence.
- **Orders ₹2,000 or Below**: For claims under or equal to ₹2,000 reported within 48 hours, photographic evidence is optional and customer service may immediately authorize a replacement or refund (`APPROVE_REFUND`).

## Resolution Options
- Once damage is verified, the customer may choose between a free replacement shipment or a full refund to the original payment method.
- Return of the physical broken item is generally not required if photographs demonstrate total loss.

## Missing Information
- If the customer reports damage but omits crucial details such as order amount, date received, or specific item damaged, customer support must request the missing details (`NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION`).
